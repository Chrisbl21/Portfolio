A LITTLE ABOUT ME

About Me

I'm Christopher Blank, a creative problem-solver interested in visual communication, thoughtful layouts, and digital experiences.

My approach

I believe good design should look polished and make information easier to understand. I enjoy taking an idea, identifying its purpose, and organizing the visual details so the final result feels clear and intentional.

I'm especially interested in branding, print materials, and digital design. I value readable typography, consistent spacing, accessible color choices, and layouts that work across different screen sizes.

What matters to me

  • Clarity: make the message easy to understand.
  • Consistency: use a visual system that feels connected.
  • Usability: design for real people and real situations.
  • Learning: keep improving through practice and feedback.
