A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Design starts with understanding.

I'm Christopher Blank. My background is in graphic design, and I'm continuing into web development because I enjoy the point where strong visuals meet useful technology.

My approach

I believe good design should look polished and make information easier to understand. I enjoy taking an idea, identifying its purpose, and organizing the visual details so the final result feels clear and intentional.

I'm drawn to automotive graphics, branding, print production, and digital design. Working around print has made me pay close attention to the details that can make or break a finished piece: file setup, scale, alignment, contrast, and legibility. I bring that same production mindset to web layouts, where responsive behavior and accessibility matter just as much.

What guides my work

  • Clarity: make the message easy to understand.
  • Consistency: use a visual system that feels connected.
  • Usability: design for real people and real situations.
  • Craft: refine the small details that make the whole piece feel intentional.
“Design is most effective when the details support the message instead of competing with it.”

A principle I bring to each project.

CURRENT DIRECTION

From print production to the browser

My work moves between physical and digital formats. A print piece has to hold up at its final size; a website has to stay clear across screens. Both demand planning, careful execution, and a willingness to revise.
