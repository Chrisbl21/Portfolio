THINKING BEHIND THE WORK

Case Studies

A strong design is more than its final appearance. It also reflects the goal, decisions, and revisions behind it.

01 · Understand

Define the goal

Identify the audience, the message, and the problem the design needs to solve before choosing a visual direction.

02 · Develop

Explore solutions

Consider layout, typography, color, and content hierarchy. Compare options against the original goal.

03 · Refine

Review the details

Check readability, consistency, accessibility, and how the design behaves at different sizes.

04 · Present

Explain the result

Show the final solution and communicate how the design choices support the audience and purpose.
