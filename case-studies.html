THINKING BEHIND THE WORK

From idea to solution.

A repeatable process helps ensure that design decisions support the audience, message, and final use.

A four-stage design process

  1. Understand the need

    Identify the audience, the message, the format, and the problem the design needs to solve.

  2. Explore directions

    Consider layout, typography, color, and content hierarchy. Compare options against the original goal.

  3. Refine the details

    Review readability, spacing, contrast, consistency, and how the design behaves at different sizes.

  4. Present and evaluate

    Explain the final decisions, gather feedback, and identify improvements for the next iteration.

Example focus · Print

Designing for quick understanding

For a printed sign, the most important information should be visible first. A clear heading, concise supporting text, and strong contrast help people understand the message at a glance.

Example focus · Web

Designing for easy navigation

For a portfolio page, consistent navigation and descriptive headings help visitors find information. Responsive layouts keep the experience useful on smaller screens.
